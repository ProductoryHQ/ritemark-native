03 — Ink ladder in practice

Four steps, four jobs.

Same body content, four tones. Notice how hierarchy reads without any color changes.

Strong ink. This is the primary tone — body copy and headings live here. High contrast, strong presence. Use for labels, H1–H3, primary text.

Body ink. The second step is where paragraph copy settles. Slightly softer than strong-ink so long passages stay readable without being heavy.

Muted ink — for captions, meta, helper text. Present but secondary. The reader knows it's there without being pulled toward it.

Faint ink. Placeholders, inactive indicators, counts. If a reader looks at this, they should feel they're looking at "supporting information" without being told.

06 — Font loading

Where the fonts come from.

Both families are loaded via Google Fonts in this skill's tokens.css. In production Ritemark, Sofia Sans is self-hosted and Space Grotesk is loaded only on moment surfaces that use it.

Sofia Sans — var(--ritemark-font-ui) Space Grotesk — var(--ritemark-font-display) Mono — var(--ritemark-font-mono)
