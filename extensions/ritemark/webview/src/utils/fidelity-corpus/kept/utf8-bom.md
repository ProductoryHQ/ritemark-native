# Proposal: customer onboarding review

Prepared for the operations team, October 2026.

## Background

Onboarding takes 34 days on average today. The target is 20.
The main delays are in contract checks and account setup.

## Scope

**1. Discovery** (weeks 1-2)

- Interview six people across sales, legal and support
- Map the current process end to end

**2. Design** (weeks 3-4)

- Propose a new process with clear owners
- Test it on two real customers

**3. Handover** (week 5)

- Written playbook
- One training session
