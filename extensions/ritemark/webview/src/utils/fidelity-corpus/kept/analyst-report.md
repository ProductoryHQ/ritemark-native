# Q3 market report

## Summary

Demand grew 6% over the quarter, led by the mid-market segment.
Prices held steady; churn fell for the third quarter in a row.

***

## Key numbers

| Metric | Q2 | Q3 | Change |
|--------|---:|---:|:------:|
| Revenue | 4.1 | 4.4 | +7% |
| New customers | 212 | 230 | +8% |
| Churn | 3.2% | 2.9% | -0.3 |

## Segments

1. **Enterprise:** flat. Two large renewals moved to Q4.
2. **Mid-market:** strongest growth, mostly in logistics.
3. **Small business:** grew, but with a higher support load.

## Method

Figures are from the billing system, rounded to one decimal.
See the appendix in `q3-appendix.xlsx` for the raw data.
