# Interviews with shift leaders

Three short interviews before Module 1, one in each warehouse. Names are left out on purpose.

## North warehouse

"I was the best picker on my shift, so they made me the leader. Nobody told me what the job actually is." Spends most of the shift covering gaps on the floor. Gives feedback only when a mistake costs money.

## South warehouse

Has led the team for eight months. Holds a short meeting at the start of each shift, but it is mostly about numbers. Would like to know how to talk to the two people on the team who are older than she is.

## East warehouse

Worried about the new shift plan, which may reach the east warehouse next year. "If I don't believe in it, how do I sell it to my team?" Wants honest answers, not a script.

## What the three have in common

- They learned the job by doing it, with no one to ask.
- Feedback means correcting mistakes.
- The change in the north warehouse is on everyone's mind.
