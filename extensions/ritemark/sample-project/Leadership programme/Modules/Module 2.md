# Module 2: Feedback that people can act on

Most feedback on the floor is either "good job" or a correction in front of others. This module gives shift leaders a short, private way to say what they saw and what they would like next time.

## Learning goals

- Give feedback in three parts: what I saw, what it led to, what I would like next time.
- Ask before giving it, and listen to the answer.
- Hold a five-minute feedback talk with each person in the team every two weeks.

## The day

**Morning.** Short input on the three parts, then practice in threes: one gives feedback, one receives it, one watches and says what worked.

**Afternoon.** Each participant plans the first round of feedback talks in their own team: who, when and where on the shift, and the first thing they will say.

## Between the modules

Hold at least four feedback talks. Note which part was hardest to say.
