# Module 1: Leading yourself

Before a shift leader can lead others, they need to see how they themselves react under pressure. This module starts there.

## Learning goals

- Notice what you do when a shift goes wrong, and what your team sees you do.
- Name one habit that helps your team, and one that gets in its way.
- Choose one small thing to try in the next three weeks.

## The day

**Morning.** The managers open the day and say, in their own words, why the company is investing in its shift leaders. Then each participant describes the hardest hour of their last month, and the group looks at what they did in that hour.

**Afternoon.** Work in pairs on a short self-review: what my team would say about me on a good day, and on a bad one. Each participant writes down one thing to try before Module 2.

## Between the modules

Try the one thing at least three times, and write two lines after each try: what happened, and what you noticed.
