# Module 3: Leading through change

Change asks more of a leader than any plan does. In this module, participants look at how they respond when the ground moves, and how their teams read that response.

## Learning goals

- Name the three common reactions to change, and spot them in a team.
- Hold a conversation about change that leaves room for doubt.
- Plan the first thirty days of a change together with the team.

## The day

**Morning.** The case: the north warehouse's new shift plan, two teams instead of three, which nobody on the floor asked for. Participants read it in pairs, then say what they would do on the first day, and what they would not say.

**Afternoon.** Each participant plans the first thirty days of a change in their own team, and gets feedback from two others. The day ends with one commitment each, written down and kept for Module 4.

## Between the modules

Have one conversation about a change with your team, and bring what you heard to Module 4.
