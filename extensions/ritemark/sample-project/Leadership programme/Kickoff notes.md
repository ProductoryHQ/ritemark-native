# Kickoff notes

With Harbour's operations director and HR lead, and the programme lead.

## The case

The north warehouse is moving to a new shift plan: two teams instead of three, one new manager, and six weeks to agree the details with the people on the floor. The director wants the shift leaders to lead this change, not only pass on the news.

## Agreed

- Six modules, one day each, one every three weeks.
- The shift plan change is the running case. Module 3 is built around it.
- Every participant leaves each module with one thing to try in their team before the next one.
- The managers join the first hour of Module 1 and the whole of Module 6.

## Not agreed yet

- Where Module 4 takes place.
- Whether the managers get a short note after each module.

## Next steps

- The programme lead drafts Modules 1 to 3 from the brief.
- HR sends the list of participants and their teams.
- The programme lead interviews three shift leaders before Module 1.
