# Brief: leadership programme for Harbour Logistics

## The client

Harbour Logistics runs three warehouses and a fleet of forty trucks. In the last year it promoted twelve people from the floor to shift leader. They know the work well. Most of them have never led a team before.

## What they want

- Shift leaders who give useful feedback every week, not only when something goes wrong.
- A real case in every module, taken from their own warehouses.
- Something their leaders can use the next morning, not a binder for the shelf.

## The programme

Six one-day modules, one every three weeks, with practice in between. Each module pairs a short input with work on a real situation from a participant's own team.

1. Leading yourself
2. Feedback that people can act on
3. Leading through change
4. Hard conversations
5. Planning the team's week
6. What I do differently now

## Who takes part

Twelve shift leaders from the three warehouses, see [Participants](Participants.csv). Their managers join the first hour of Module 1 and all of Module 6.

## What success looks like

After six months, the shift leaders hold a short feedback talk with each person in their team every two weeks, and the team survey's question "My shift leader tells me how I am doing" moves from 2.9 to 3.8 out of 5.

## Open questions

- Which warehouse hosts Module 4? The training room in the north warehouse is booked for most of the autumn.
- Do the managers want a short report after each module, or only at the end?
