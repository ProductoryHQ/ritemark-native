# About this project

A sample project in Ritemark: a leadership programme for Harbour Logistics, a fictional company. Try things here; nothing in it is real.

## What this project is

Six one-day modules for twelve new shift leaders. The brief is in Brief.md, what the client agreed is in Kickoff notes.md, and the modules are in the Modules folder.

## How to write here

- Plain, short sentences. The readers are shift leaders, not HR specialists.
- Each module follows the same shape: a short opening, learning goals, the day (morning and afternoon), and what to do between the modules.
- Use real situations from the warehouses, from Kickoff notes.md and the Research folder. Do not make up numbers.

## What not to change

- Participants.csv is kept by HR. Read it, but do not change it unless asked.
- Do not send anything outside this folder.
