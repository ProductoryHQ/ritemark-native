# 06. Technical Debt Triage

## Objective

Turn quality findings into decisions instead of letting audit reports accumulate.

## Triage Fields

- Finding.
- Risk level.
- Affected area.
- Business impact.
- Recommended fix.
- Estimated effort.
- Decision: fix now, schedule, monitor, or reject.
- Owner.

## First Implementation

- Create a lightweight triage template.
- Review findings once per sprint.
- Only promote findings that have a clear risk or business impact.
- Keep rejected or monitored findings visible but low-friction.

## Starting Checklist

- [ ] Draft triage template.
- [ ] Apply it to first audit findings.
- [ ] Decide threshold for sprint inclusion.
- [ ] Define owner assignment.
- [ ] Review stale findings monthly.

## Automation Later

- Finding database or structured markdown index.
- Deduplication of repeated findings.
- Risk trend report.
- Suggested sprint backlog items from accepted findings.
