# 10. Management Reporting

## Objective

Frame quality assurance as business risk management.

## Reporting Questions

- Are release risks going down?
- Are regressions decreasing?
- Are security-sensitive changes getting proper review?
- Are critical flows covered by tests?
- Are repeated findings being fixed or ignored?
- Is development becoming easier or more fragile?

## First Implementation

- Produce a short monthly quality summary.
- Include only metrics that lead to decisions.
- Highlight top risks, accepted risks, and completed improvements.
- Keep the report founder-readable.

## Starting Checklist

- [ ] Define 5 initial quality indicators.
- [ ] Create monthly summary template.
- [ ] Link indicators to audit and triage outputs.
- [ ] Review whether the report changes priorities.

## Automation Later

- Monthly quality digest.
- Risk trend chart.
- Release readiness score.
- AI-generated executive summary from validated findings.
