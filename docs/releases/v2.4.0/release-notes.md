# Ritemark 2.4.0

You can now keep a published deck up to date as you save it.

If you run training or share slides with clients, you can update the page they already have. You can see Excel charts and colour rules as you review a workbook. You can also ask AI to read your project documents and follow links to its sources.

<!-- screenshot: A deck in Ritemark beside its published page, showing an edit on the same slide after saving -->

## Keep a published deck up to date

- **Update your page on every save.** After you publish, turn on **Update on every save** for that page. On older pages, choose **Update the page** once so open pages can follow your changes.
- **Let readers stay on their slide.** When readers pause, their page catches up and keeps them on the same slide if it's still there.
- **See what happens after an AI edit.** When the AI saves your live deck, chat tells you whether the page updated or why it paused.
- **See if your slides fit the session.** In **Timed slide list**, set each slide’s minutes and compare the total with your session length. Your timings stay private when you publish.

## See Excel charts and cell colours

<!-- screenshot: An Excel workbook in Ritemark with a chart beside cells showing colour rules and data bars -->

- **See charts where they belong.** Column, bar, line, area, pie, doughnut and scatter charts appear in place, including on their own sheets. Charts update when you edit values they use.
- **See charts with two scales.** A chart that combines columns and a line can show a separate scale for each.
- **Copy a chart as a picture.** Click a chart, or press Cmd+Option+5 on a Mac or Ctrl+Alt+5 on Windows, then copy it to paste into a document or a message.
- **See which cells meet your rules.** Highlights, colour scales and data bars appear in the workbook. They change as you edit values.

## Ask AI about your project documents

<!-- screenshot: An AI answer with a named Word source and its quoted passage open beside the chat -->

- **Ask about Word, PDF and PowerPoint files.** Claude, ChatGPT and OpenCode can read those files in your project. Ask for specific PDF pages or about a PowerPoint slide and its speaker notes.
- **Check where an answer came from.** Hover over a named source to see the passage. Open the link to read the passage or go to the cited slide.

## Smaller changes

- **Keep Word formulas as math.** They carry over when AI reads the document or you make a Markdown copy. Some formulas look different.
- **Share pictures without private details.** Ritemark removes location, camera and author details from the pictures it publishes.
- **See how many slides you have.** The bottom of the window shows the slide count when you open a deck.

## Fixes

- **Copy cells from an Excel workbook.** Copying cells in Ritemark's Excel view now puts their text on the clipboard.
- **Attach Office files from chat.** The paperclip now accepts Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.
- **Hear button names on a phone.** Screen readers now announce the More and Contents buttons by name on published deck pages.

## Updating from 2.3

- **On a Mac,** choose **Download Installer** when Ritemark offers the update. Open the download and drag Ritemark to Applications.
- **On Windows,** choose **Download Installer** if you installed from a download. If you installed from the Microsoft Store, update there.
- Your project files, chats and settings stay in place.

## Known issues

- **Some charts still need Excel.** Open the workbook in Excel to see charts such as waterfall, treemap and histogram charts.
- **Cells with icon rules show their values.** Open the workbook in Excel to see icons such as arrows or traffic lights.
- **Ask your document question in chat.** To get started with a Word file or PDF, type your question directly.
- **Look for document names in the answer.** The steps shown while the AI reads may leave out file names.
