# Landing-page copy — Comments (v1.8.3)

## Headline

**Comment on your document, without touching the words.**

### Subhead

Select any passage, drop a note in the margin, and keep your prose clean — the way you'd expect from a modern document editor, now in Ritemark.

### Benefit bullets

- **Margin notes, Google-Docs style.** Select text and click **Comment** to highlight the passage and open a note beside it. Type `///` anywhere for a quick standalone note in the right margin.
- **Notes that stay with the file.** Comments save straight into your Markdown, so they're still there when you reopen the document — no sidecar files, no lost annotations.
- **Annotate without cluttering the prose.** Questions, reminders, "rework this" flags — they live in the margin, not in your text.

### AI assignment line

Mention `@claude`, `@codex`, or `@opencode` in a comment and hit **Send to AI** — Ritemark hands that exact passage, plus your note, straight to the AI sidebar. Comment where you're already looking; the agent gets the full context.
