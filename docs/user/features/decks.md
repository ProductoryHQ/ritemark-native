# Decks

Use a deck to put slides and a handout together. Choose **New → Deck** to start one. Press **Present** on the deck's bar to show your slides.

## A page that follows its deck

Choose **Publish** on the deck's bar to share a page. Once it's published, open the **Published** menu and turn on **Update on every save**. The switch starts off for each new page. With it off, **Published · changed** means you have changes to share. Choose **Update the page** when you're ready.

With the switch on, the chip says **Live** with a green dot. While the page is updating, a spinner takes the dot's place. If Ritemark can't reach the page, the chip says **Offline** in grey. Your saves stay on your computer, and Ritemark tries the latest one again while the deck is open.

Open pages usually catch up in about 15 seconds, once the reader stops moving or finishes selecting text. Readers stay on their slide and keep their ticks. If their slide changes, the changed text is briefly highlighted and **Updated just now** appears in the page's top bar. If their slide is taken out, they can finish reading it and choose **Go on to** the slide named there. For a page published before 2.4, use **Update the page** once to give it this behaviour.

### When an update pauses

An amber **Paused** chip means readers keep the version they already have. Open the chip to see why. Ritemark can pause when it finds what looks like a key, a file outside the deck's folder, a file over 25 MB, more than 20,000 files, or a picture it couldn't clean. It can also pause if it can't check a file, if the page has moved somewhere Ritemark can't use it, or if you need to connect again.

When the menu names a slide, choose **Show it on slide** to open it. After you deal with the issue, choose **Go on** to check and send the latest saved version. If the key is still there, take it out first. If a file is outside the deck's folder but inside the project, **Go on** can accept the files named in the pause. Files still have to fit the limits. Saving again leaves the pause in place.

Ritemark removes location, camera and author details from the pictures it publishes. Your original pictures stay as they are.

After an AI saves a live deck, look below its answer for **Updated the page** or **The page waits** with the reason. A change to slide or session times alone stays in the deck.

## A deck's timetable

Click the minutes after the slide count on the deck's bar ("Slide 1 of 6 · 5 min"), type the minutes for this slide, and press Return. Escape keeps the old minutes, and an empty box takes them away. The thumbnails show the minutes too. A section shows the sum of its slides. If an exercise label already says “20 min,” Ritemark uses that time until you enter your own.

Choose **Timed slide list** at the top of the slide strip to see each slide's start and minutes. Enter a number and press Return to move to the next row. The slide on the stage follows you.

Set the session's **Start** and **Length** at the bottom of the list. With a start set, you can edit the end time to change the length. The rows show clock times when you set a start, and time from the beginning otherwise. The status bar and list show the total and how far it is over or under the session. The presenter view also uses the session length. Your timetable stays in the deck.
