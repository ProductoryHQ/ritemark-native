Transcribe

An audio transcription app inside Ritemark: drop a recording, get markdown you can work with — on-device with Whisper, or with ElevenLabs Scribe when you need to know who said what.

Three UI options below. They are different products, not three skins on the same one. Pick the bet you believe in; the sprint plan follows from it.

Two facts that shape all three options.

1. On-device Whisper cannot separate speakers. whisper.cpp only offers experimental English-only turn markers. Real diarization — up to 32 speakers, per-word attribution — means sending the audio to ElevenLabs. So “private” and “knows who spoke” are, today, mutually exclusive. Every option has to make that trade legible instead of hiding it.

2. On-device Whisper is macOS-only. The bundled binary is darwin-arm64 only (issue #133). On Windows, Transcribe is ElevenLabs-only until we ship a Windows Whisper build.
