# Sprint 167 spec

The README is the spec. It lives in the governance repo at `product/design/experience/experiences/decks/README.md`, section "Five small things from Jarmo's rundev test" (ritemark-dev commit 9f05294), with the sketches in `block-details.html` beside it. This file only numbers the requirements so the scenarios and tasks can point at them. Where this file and the README differ, the README wins.

- **R1 (README item A).** Slide settings has a Content row, Top, Middle or Bottom, as tiles like Picture's. The whole flow moves together, title included. Top is the default. Title and section slides read as Bottom and can be changed: they stay drawn low exactly as today, Bottom is only their default value (Slide settings shows it chosen), nothing is written until another value is picked, and picking Bottom again removes the setting; for a title slide Bottom means today's drawing with its own foot room, on other slides it puts the content at the flow's foot; the theme holds no positions (the Experience lead, 19 November). A full slide looks the same in all three, and the fit is unchanged. With a Left or Right picture the content aligns in its column, with Behind it moves over the picture, and Columns keep their own alignment. In the file it is a slide setting, written only when it isn't the default. The wall, the page, the strip and the presenter view draw it alike.
- **R2 (README item B).** On a chosen block, the item under the pointer or cursor shows a small handle in the middle of its top edge, shaped like the Text editor's table column grip. Menu: Duplicate, Move left, Move right, Delete box. A drag moves it, with a drop line. Columns have the same handle (Duplicate, Move left, Move right, Delete column). Steps and list items have it on their left edge with Move up and Move down. Backspace in an empty item still removes it. The handle shows only on the chosen block and never covers words.
- **R3 (README item C).** A Boxes block's bar gains Mark: Icon, Number, Dot, Arrow, after Filled and Outline, as a menu that shows its value. They map to the existing looks (boxes, steps, line, arrows, with `OWN_MARK_LOOKS` and the default). Icon is the default and keeps each box's own icon. Number follows the order through moves, adds and deletes.
- **R4 (README item D, empty place).** No centred prompt. A corner chip "Picture behind · Choose… · ChatGPT" sits top right, or bottom right when the theme's logo is top right. At rest it is only a small round picture handle, like the block's white handles, and it opens into the chip while the pointer is over the slide. It never covers words or takes their clicks. Choosing Behind with no picture opens the chooser at once. A paste or drop anywhere on the slide goes behind.
- **R5 (README item D, with a picture).** A click where there are no words chooses the picture. The ring goes round the whole slide and the picture bar opens: Focus (dot), In text, Left, Right, Behind, Description, ChatGPT and ⋯ (Replace picture…, Copy, Remove). No Size or Crop for Behind. At rest nothing is in the corner. On hover the chip "Picture behind · Choose" appears and chooses the picture. A paste or drop on the chosen picture replaces it. Slide settings shows Replace… and Remove under Behind.
- **R6 (README item E).** The theme's Where becomes two segments, Top | Bottom and Left | Right, giving four corners, bottom left included. The slide makes room for the logo in any corner, so words never run under it. Bottom left matters most on title slides.
- **R7 (HARD RULE #1).** Replacing D's centred prompt with the chip keeps its function, so it is not a removal. Anything else that goes is listed in the sprint plan and waits for Jarmo's yes by name.
