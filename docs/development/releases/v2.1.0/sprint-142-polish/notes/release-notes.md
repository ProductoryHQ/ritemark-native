# Sprint 142: release notes draft (applied at 2.1's closeout)

## Polish

- **Lists in the AI's answers have their bullets and numbers back.** When the AI offers two things it could do next, the numbers are there to answer with.
- **Split buttons look like one button.** Home's first-day card, Give to and Retry with on tasks, dictation, and Open in Word and Excel: one colour, one hover, and never half of a button greyed out. Pressed a second time, the first-day card takes you back to the chat it started instead of doing nothing.
- **The note after you switch projects steps aside.** It goes after a few seconds, or as soon as you start something, and never covers "Drop files here".
- **Home shows a long folder path cut in the middle,** keeping your project's own folder whole, with the whole path in its tooltip.
- **A file you transcribe opens as a tab,** and its transcript fills in there, as a recording made in Ritemark does.
- **The Task board fits a 1440-wide window** with both side columns showing.
- **File changes is its name everywhere:** in More, at the head of its panel, in the command palette and in View ▸ Advanced.
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - no white strip beside the left column's scrolling lists on Windows;
  - a tooltip no longer stays up after a click;
  - Home fits even the narrowest window with the AI's sheet open: when the folder's name is the project's name, its line shows just the folder, and a button too narrow for its words has them in its tooltip;
  - cleaner edges on the AI's sheet and the desk;
  - a task's tab shows the task's title after a restart, not its file's name;
  - Open in Excel looks like Open in Word.
