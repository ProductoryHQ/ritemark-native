# Chart reader proof

The table records the parsed models from the 21 synthetic chart parts, the two existing workbooks and 24 charts from the Experience lead’s Excel-made workbooks. Ranges list the series title, categories or X values, and values or Y values, in that order. Each line break starts a new series. An unsupported model reports its kind and has no parsed series. The file's drawing card still supplies the preview in this PR.

Regenerate the rows from `extensions/ritemark` with `node --import tsx webview/src/components/excel/readChart.test.ts --proof`. The PNGs are in the Experience fixture set’s `reference/` directory. The comparison checks model metadata and series or slice colours against Excel’s captures. For the colour fixtures, PNG samples were converted from the embedded display profile to sRGB, with up to one RGB step of capture rounding.
